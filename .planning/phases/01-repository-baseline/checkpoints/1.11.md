## Remaining risks

- The guard is module-level: a module selected through `file::test` nodes can still leave functions unrun (for example the six non-native functions of `integration/tests/test_results.py`, and the three sets above). A node-level guard would need each partial registration's exclusions listed.
- `public-api` and `modeling-contract` load session fixtures through `pytest_plugins` in test modules; if a later pytest removes that mechanism, those groups fail loudly (missing fixture), they do not pass silently.
- `native-execution` takes about 6.5 minutes; `qualification` grows by about 12 minutes overall.
