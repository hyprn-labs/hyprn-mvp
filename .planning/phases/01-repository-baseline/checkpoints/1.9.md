- **Fence exemption.** The scratch root below the data root is now writable by scoped tests (only when it overlaps no store, evidence folder, read-only location or live root). Review against T-1-04.
- **Documents widenings** (review item above).
- **Superseded fixture** `native-20261013T133537Z` and the two older fixture folders remain below `<fixtures>` unpinned (about 0.9 GB together with the pinned one); deletion is the product owner's decision.
- **Producer reproducibility.** A new capture needs the pinned modeling producer image, the engine base image, Docker, Node/Playwright and about 13 minutes; worker images are not bit-reproducible, so a later continuation on a re-materialized copy rebuilds and re-attests the worker at stage `baselined`.
- **Human acceptance** of the fixture runs is pending; automated browser evidence is not human acceptance.
