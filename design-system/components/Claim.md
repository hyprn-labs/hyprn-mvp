# Claim

ClaimCard is a reading composition over existing domain records, never a Claim API. It keeps full text, domain kind, explicit scope, actual limits, attribution and basis together. Scientific answers offer Inspect answer basis without counting observations or interpretations as claims. Their basis is labeled answer-wide; per-passage associations are unavailable. Every attached evidence retains its own direct View evidence action. Source selection pushes the exact retained reader into the same panel; Back restores group, statement, scroll and invoking focus.
