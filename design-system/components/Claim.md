# Claim

ClaimCard is a reading composition over existing domain records, never a Claim API. It keeps full text, domain kind, explicit scope, actual limits, attribution and basis together. ClaimButton accepts owner/version, stable statement IDs, coverage state and label. Distinct item IDs count once each even with identical text or shared sources; evidence and prose fields are not statement counts. Complete empty, partial and unknown sets remain distinct. Scientific answers offer Inspect answer basis without counting observations or interpretations as claims. Their basis is labeled answer-wide; per-passage associations are unavailable. Every attached evidence retains its own direct View evidence action. Source selection pushes the exact retained reader into the same panel; Back restores group, statement, scroll and invoking focus.
